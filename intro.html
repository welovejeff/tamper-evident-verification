🎯 The Luhn Algorithm

Here's how the computer checks your number:

4 0 2 4 6 0 7 2 3 6 9 5 0 7 4 8

Step 1: Starting from the right, we double every second digit...

✖️ Step 2: Double every other digit

Start with the credit card number (excluding check digit):
4 0 2 4 6 0 7 2 3 6 9 5 0 7 4
Double the value of every other digit (highlighted in blue)
↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓
4 0 2 8 6 0 7 4 3 12 9 10 0 14 4

🔧 Step 3: Fix numbers bigger than 9

If any doubled result is bigger than 9, add its digits together:
12 → 1 + 2 = 3
10 → 1 + 0 = 1
14 → 1 + 4 = 5
Now all our digits are single digits:
4 0 2 8 6 0 7 4 3 3 9 1 0 5 4

➕ Step 4: Add all resulting digits together

Add all the digits from our result:
4 + 0 + 2 + 8 + 6 + 0 + 7 + 4 + 3 + 3 + 9 + 1 + 0 + 5 + 4 = 67
Add the check digit to the sum
↓
67 + 8 = 75

🏁 The Final Test

If the result is not a multiple of 10, the number is invalid
Is 75 divisible by 10?
75 ÷ 10 = 7 remainder 5
❌ NOT VALID
